How it works

Three simple steps to a safer scan.

1

Scan

Upload a QR code image. QRShield decodes it and reads the link hidden inside — no app or camera needed.

2

Analyze

The link is checked against eleven common phishing patterns: fake domains, disguised characters, shortened links, and more.

3

Understand

You get a plain risk score, exactly which checks passed or failed, and a clear recommendation on whether to proceed.

Built for awareness

Don't let a QR code hide the destination.

QR codes make links convenient, but they can also hide suspicious destinations. QRShield lets you check the link first, entirely offline, before you ever open it.
